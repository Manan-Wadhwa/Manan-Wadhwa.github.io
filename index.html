Home CV Resume: for humans · for clankers

I'm a final-year computer science undergraduate at MAIT in Delhi, graduating in 2027. I work on AI safety, mostly on one question: when a method claims to read what's inside a model, how would we know if it's wrong? I build models where the answer is known in advance (a habit installed, hidden, or never there) and check whether the instruments find it. Usually they don't, and some of my favourite results are false positives we caught in our own work.

I co-founded Redarc Labs, a nonprofit that studies what deployed AI systems can do that their models alone can't. I'm also a research fellow at MARS (Cambridge AI Safety Hub), working on model organisms with hidden objectives, and a special projects fellow at Secure AI Futures Lab, which works on trustworthy AI for India and South Asia. This summer I was a Google Summer of Code fellow with HumanAI, writing pathfinding algorithms to work out how people moved through the El Bagawat necropolis in Egypt.
